# after genesis

Before Existence, there was the Void; the Universal Mind.

Mind created a thought-form, and the Universe came into being.

Some say we are projections of Mind, incomplete though equal. Others say we are facets on the diamond that is Mind; some at the top, some at the bottom.

And bloody wars are fought.

Although we will not speak our truth, there is comfort in not believing. When we end, it ends. 

And we all return to the Void.
