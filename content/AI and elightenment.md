## just some thoughts

Agree or disagree, it's refreshing to read a different take on AI instead of dystopian doom. Extrapolating from the article, it's interesting to think of ways to use technology spiritually. What would actual technoshamans be like? What would their practice look like?

To me, shamanism and nature have always been inextricably linked. Being an animist, I'm trying to find a reason why technology could not be a part of shamanism. After all, isn't it a natural part of evolution to change the way we define what is meaningful? Sacred? Maybe in some distant future we'll look back at this time and realize this is when we sow the seeds of a new religion. 

Or maybe we will have become so dependent on AI that a single power outage destroyed our society.
